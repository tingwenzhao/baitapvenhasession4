A website( also written as a web site)is one or more web pages and related content that is indentified by a commom domain name and published on at least one web sever. Websites are typically delicated to aparticular topic or purpose, such as new, education, commerce, entertainment,or social media. Hyperlinking between web pages guides the navigation of the site, which often starts with a home page. The most-visited sites are Google, Youtube, and Facebook.

All publiccly-accessible websites collectively constitute the World Wide Web. There are also private websites that can only be accessed on a private network, such as a company's internal websites for its employees. User can access websites on a range of devices, including destops, laptop, tablet, and smartphone. The app used on these devices is called a web browser.
